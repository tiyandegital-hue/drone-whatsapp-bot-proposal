Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. This page covers the concept proposal site you're reading.

What we collect

Almost nothing. This demo site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and stores nothing about your visit. The interactive demo runs entirely in your browser — your taps never leave your device.

The contact form

The form on the Contact page is a non-functional demo in this proposal build. In a live deployment it would collect your name, email, and message only to reply to your inquiry, with your explicit consent (the checkbox). We would never sell, rent, or share your details, and you can ask for deletion at any time via the contact email on our main site.

Third parties

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log technical data (like your IP address) when serving them — that's outside our control; see Google's privacy policy.
