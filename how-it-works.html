Architecture

Six parts.
Zero re-typing.

Every message, number, and total has exactly one place it lives. Here's the whole system, end to end.

1

WhatsApp Business API

The front door. Clients message your existing business number; every message arrives via official API webhooks. Template messages for proactive follow-ups, approved by Meta.

↓
2

Conversation layer

Understands free-text answers ("six hours, thermal too") and falls back to quick-tap buttons. Anything ambiguous goes to you — the bot never guesses a price.

↓
3

Drive template engine

Pulls the right client template from Google Drive and fills it: name, hours, line items, terms. Your templates stay the source of truth — edit a doc, the bot follows.

↓
4

Quoting engine — computed, not guessed

Flight hours × your rate card, extras added, invoice number sequenced from a counter that can't repeat. Arithmetic is deterministic; AI never touches the math.

↓
5

Sheets ledger

Every confirmed invoice appends one row: number, client, hours, total, timestamp. Your books update the second you tap confirm — no night-time re-typing.

↓
6

Repeat-client learning

Each finished job is logged against the client. Next quote starts from their real history — average rate, usual extras — so returning clients get sharper quotes automatically.

Design decisions

Boring where it matters.

AI talks, math computes

The language model handles conversation. Prices, invoice numbers, and totals run through strict code paths. A wrong total is a lost client — so totals can't be wrong.

Your tools stay yours

Drive and Sheets remain the system of record. The bot reads and writes them; it never replaces them. If the bot ever goes down, your data is untouched.

Human in the loop

Every quote waits for your tap before it sends. Full auto-send is possible later — we start with you in control.
